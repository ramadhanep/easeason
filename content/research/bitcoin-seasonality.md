# Bitcoin Seasonality and the Election Cycle

Bitcoin trades every day of the year, including weekends, which means its seasonal profile fills the full 365-day range rather than skipping non-trading days.

That also means its daily-compounded path can differ noticeably from equities, which carry fewer observations per year.
