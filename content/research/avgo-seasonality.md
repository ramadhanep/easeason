# AVGO Seasonality Through the Election Cycle

Broadcom Inc has traded publicly since 2010, giving us a 16-year sample to break down by the U.S. presidential election cycle. The data shows consistent seasonal patterns across different election-year profiles.

## Reading the chart

The "All Years" line shows the average cumulative path across all valid years. The election-cycle profiles break those same years into groups by year type:

- **Election** — year % 4 == 0
- **Post-Election** — year % 4 == 1
- **Mid-Term** — year % 4 == 2
- **Pre-Election** — year % 4 == 3
- **Trump Years** — the defined Trump-administration years (2016–2019, 2024–2025)
- **2026 YTD** — the direct cumulative return of the current year, so far

## Highlights

- **2026 is running ahead of the historical pace.** Through late April, AVGO is up **+20.3%**, above the ~14.1% average "All Years" cumulative gain at the same point. However, it trails the Pre-Election profile by about **6.8 percentage points**.
- **Post-Election years are the strongest profile**, finishing near **+93%** cumulative by year-end, with Mid-Term years the laggard at **+56%**.
- **Post-Election years show a sharp acceleration in Q4**, climbing from ~70% in October to ~93% by year-end.
- **2026 saw a notable divergence in June**, dropping from **+32.6%** in May to just **+6.6%** in June — a reversal not typical in most election-cycle profiles.

## Caveats

This is historical statistics, not a forecast. Averages smooth over individual outlier years, so trade the summary above with that in mind. Full daily data is available on the [explorer page](/explore/AVGO).
