# AAPL Seasonality Through the Election Cycle

Apple has traded publicly since December 1980, giving us 45 years of history to break down by the U.S. presidential election cycle — one of the deepest samples in the curated list.

## Reading the chart

The "All Years" line shows the average cumulative path across all valid years. The election-cycle profiles break those same years into groups by year type:

- **Election** — year % 4 == 0
- **Post-Election** — year % 4 == 1
- **Mid-Term** — year % 4 == 2
- **Pre-Election** — year % 4 == 3
- **Trump Years** — the defined Trump-administration years (2016–2019, 2024–2025)
- **2026 YTD** — the direct cumulative return of the current year, so far

## Highlights

- **2026 is trailing the historical pace.** The last confirmed 2026 data point (late August) puts AAPL up about **+18.3%** year-to-date, well below the ~31% average cumulative gain "All Years" typically shows by that point.
- **Pre-Election years are the strongest seasonal tailwind by far**, building a lead early and finishing near **+94%** cumulative by year-end on average.
- **Election years are the seasonal laggard**, spending most of the year in single digits and finishing around **+16%** — the weakest of all profiles.
- **Post-Election years accelerate late**, sitting near flat through mid-year before jumping to +31% by November.

## Caveats

This is historical statistics, not a forecast. Averages smooth over individual outlier years, so trade the summary above with that in mind. Full daily data is available on the [explorer page](/explore/AAPL).
