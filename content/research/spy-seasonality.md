# SPY Seasonality Through the Election Cycle

The SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust has tracked the S&P 500 since early 1993, giving us 33 years of history to break down by the U.S. presidential election cycle.

## Reading the chart

The "All Years" line shows the average cumulative path across all valid years. The election-cycle profiles break those same years into groups by year type:

- **Election** — year % 4 == 0
- **Post-Election** — year % 4 == 1
- **Mid-Term** — year % 4 == 2
- **Pre-Election** — year % 4 == 3
- **Trump Years** — the defined Trump-administration years (2016–2019, 2024–2025)
- **2026 YTD** — the direct cumulative return of the current year, so far

## Highlights

- **Mid-Term years dip in the middle.** SPY turns negative through the spring and summer (bottoming near **-3.8%** at end of September) before recovering to finish around **+4.4%** — the weakest profile, but still green.
- **Pre-Election years are the strongest tailwind**, steadily climbing toward a **+28.4%** cumulative finish on average.
- **Trump Years run hottest**, holding near the top of the pack all year and closing around **+30.9%**.
- **2026 opened weak** (SPY down to **-3.8%** at the end of March) before a sharp recovery through spring, hitting **+9.7%** cumulative by the end of June — the most recent confirmed 2026 data point.

## Caveats

This is historical statistics, not a forecast. Averages smooth over individual outlier years — both the 2000–02 bear market and the 2008 crash sit inside this sample, and mid-term years in particular can swing widely. Full daily data is available on the [explorer page](/explore/SPY).
