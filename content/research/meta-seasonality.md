# META Seasonality Through the Election Cycle

Meta Platforms has traded publicly since 2013, giving us a 13-year sample to break down by the U.S. presidential election cycle. That covers at least three full cycles across every profile.

## Reading the chart

The "All Years" line shows the average cumulative path across all valid years. The election-cycle profiles break those same years into groups by year type:

- **Election** — year % 4 == 0
- **Post-Election** — year % 4 == 1
- **Mid-Term** — year % 4 == 2
- **Pre-Election** — year % 4 == 3
- **Trump Years** — the defined Trump-administration years (2016–2019, 2024–2025)
- **2026 YTD** — the direct cumulative return of the current year, so far

## Highlights

- **2026 is well below the historical pace.** Through August, META is down **-11.9%**, far behind the ~54% average cumulative gain "All Years" typically shows by that point. It is also negative versus the Post-Election profile, which averages **+63.7%** by August.
- **Pre-Election years are by far the strongest**, compounding to **+149%** by December. The second half of Pre-Election years is especially strong, nearly doubling from ~99% in August to ~149% by year-end.
- **Mid-Term years are the weakest by a wide margin**, posting losses throughout the entire year and finishing at **-31.1%**. No other profile spends the full year underwater.
- **2026's deepest trough came in late March**, hitting **-19.1%** on Mar 26, before recovering to **-5.6%** by June 30. The second half has seen renewed weakness, falling back to **-14.3%** by late July.

## Caveats

This is historical statistics, not a forecast. Averages smooth over individual outlier years, so trade the summary above with that in mind. Full daily data is available on the [explorer page](/explore/META).
